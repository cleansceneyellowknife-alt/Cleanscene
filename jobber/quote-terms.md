# Standard quote terms (draft, for Adam to approve)

Paste into Jobber > Settings > Quotes > Contract / Disclaimer. Approving the quote online is the client's signature on these terms.

1. **Scope.** We clean what is listed on this quote. Extra work is quoted separately before we do it.
2. **Access.** The client provides access at the booked time (keys, codes, alarm, parking). If we cannot get in, a lockout fee equal to 50% of the visit applies.
3. **Rescheduling and cancellation.** At least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel. Less notice: 50% of the visit is charged.
4. **Pets and valuables.** Please secure pets and put away cash, jewellery and fragile valuables.
5. **Satisfaction.** Tell us within 24 hours if something was missed and we will come back to re-clean that area at no charge.
6. **Damage.** Report damage within 48 hours. We are insured and will repair or replace items damaged by our negligence. We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, normal wear, or items that were not secured.
7. **Supplies.** We bring our own supplies and equipment unless agreed otherwise.
8. **Payment.** Residential: due on receipt. Commercial: net 15 from the invoice date. Overdue balances may pause service.
9. **Recurring service.** Recurring rates require the agreed frequency. Either party may end recurring service with 14 days' notice (commercial: 30 days, or as set out in the master agreement).
10. **Non-solicitation.** The client agrees not to hire CleanScene staff directly for 12 months after their last visit.
11. **Health and safety.** Our staff may refuse or stop work in unsafe conditions (biohazards, aggressive animals, unsafe structures). The visit is rescheduled and charged as a cancellation if the hazard was not disclosed.

Commercial clients with a signed master agreement: the master agreement prevails where it differs.
